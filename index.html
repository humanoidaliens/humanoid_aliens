01 — INTRODUCTION

Are We Alone in the Universe?

Earth is currently the only planet known to support life. However, scientists have discovered thousands of planets outside our solar system. Could some of these distant worlds support living organisms?

The Main Research Question

This investigation explores whether humanoid extraterrestrial beings could exist, which planets might support life and what scientists have discovered so far.

It is important to separate scientific evidence from theories and speculation.

02 — ALIEN BIOLOGY

What Are Humanoid Aliens?

Understanding Humanoid Life

Humanoid describes a creature with a body structure resembling a human. This could include an upright body, two arms, two legs and a head.

There is currently no confirmed scientific evidence that humanoid extraterrestrial beings exist. The illustrations on this website are not photographs or scientific evidence.

Could Aliens Evolve Like Humans?

Life on another planet could develop very differently from life on Earth. Different gravity, temperatures and environments could affect how organisms evolve.

It is possible to imagine human-like organisms, but scientists cannot currently predict what extraterrestrial life would look like.

03 — HABITABLE WORLDS

Could Other Planets Support Life?

Scientists search for exoplanets. These are planets that orbit stars outside our solar system.

What Is the Habitable Zone?

The habitable zone is the region around a star where temperatures could allow liquid water to exist on a planet's surface, depending on its atmosphere.

Being inside this zone does not automatically mean a planet contains life.

04 — SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE

What Has NASA Discovered?

Thousands of Exoplanets

NASA has confirmed thousands of planets beyond our solar system. Some have characteristics that make them interesting targets for further research.

Explore NASA's Exoplanets

James Webb Space Telescope

The James Webb Space Telescope studies distant planets and their atmospheres. Scientists can investigate chemicals in the light passing through these atmospheres.

These observations may help scientists understand whether certain environments could support life.

NASA: James Webb Mission

Searching for Biosignatures

Biosignatures are possible clues that life may exist. Scientists investigate chemicals and patterns that could be produced by biological activity.

However, individual chemicals are not proof of life. Scientists must rule out non-biological explanations.

05 — FINAL INVESTIGATION

Conclusion: Do Humanoid Aliens Exist?

Based on current scientific evidence, we cannot confirm whether humanoid aliens exist.

The discovery of thousands of exoplanets shows that planets are common throughout our galaxy. Some may have conditions suitable for life, but this does not prove that life exists on them.

Even if life exists elsewhere, it may be very different from humans. More scientific research and observations are needed to answer this question.
